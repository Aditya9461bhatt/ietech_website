## What i.e tech does

i.e tech offers **i.e ERP** — a single, intelligent platform that runs an entire restaurant or hotel
business end to end. Instead of juggling spreadsheets, sticky notes, and disconnected apps, operators
manage the point of sale, kitchen, tables, rooms, guests, housekeeping, accounting, and inventory
from one connected system.

It is built specifically for **restaurant chains and hotel groups** that have outgrown standalone,
disconnected tools and need everything — front desk to back office, across multiple outlets — working
off the same live data.

## The unified workflow — "Experience the workflow"

The platform is presented as **a unified flow for operations** across three connected surfaces. An
order or a guest moves through all three without data ever being re-entered.

### 1. Point of Sale (POS)
*Watch a live order flow — from menu browsing to payment, all in one screen.*

- **Multi-payment support**
- **GST compliant**
- **Real-time analytics**
- **KOT printing** (Kitchen Order Tickets)

The POS handles item groups and menu browsing, add-to-cart, discounts, walk-in customers, automatic
subtotal / discount / grand-total calculation, and checkout — with recent orders and new-invoice
creation built in.

### 2. Restaurant Desk
*Visual table management, instant reservation updates, and KOT generation directly from the desk.*

- **Table status** — live view of every table (Available, Occupied, Reserved, Cleaning)
- **Active order tracking** — see the running invoice and elapsed time per occupied table
- **Reservation management** — seat, reserve, and cancel from one board
- **Revenue snapshot** — totals per table and outlet at a glance

The table board spans multiple outlets and seating areas (indoor, bar) across properties, so a chain
sees every table in every location on one screen.

### 3. Hotel Front Desk — Hotel Overview
*Track room occupancies, manage check-ins, and monitor housekeeping seamlessly.*

- **Room overviews** — a visual room board (Occupied, Reserved, Available, Maintenance, plus
  housekeeping states like Clean, Inspected, Dirty, Out of Order)
- **Check-in flows** — check guests in and out directly from the board
- **Housekeeping status** — per-room cleaning and maintenance state in real time
- **Guest management** — bookings, room folio, guest profiles, and an operations center with reporting

## How it operates

The strength of i.e ERP is that the modules are connected, so one action updates everything downstream:

1. **An order is taken at the POS** — items, discounts, and payment handled on one screen, GST-compliant.
2. **The kitchen is notified instantly** — a digital Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT) prints/displays
   directly to the kitchen, removing paper tickets and miscommunication.
3. **The Restaurant Desk keeps tables in sync** — table status, reservations, and active orders update
   live across every outlet.
4. **The Hotel Front Desk manages the stay** — room occupancy, check-in/check-out, folio billing, and
   housekeeping status all reflect in real time.
5. **Guest data follows the guest** — preferences, stay history, and loyalty points build a unified
   profile that powers personalized marketing.
6. **Finance reconciles itself** — POS activity flows automatically into the accounting ledger, with
   daily revenue reconciliation and tax-compliant GST invoicing.
7. **Inventory stays ahead** — minimum stock levels are tracked, purchase requests are automated, and
   stock is managed across multiple storage locations.

The result is one intelligent platform replacing a stack of disconnected tools — from kitchen to
cloud, front desk to back office, across every outlet.
