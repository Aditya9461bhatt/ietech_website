# The Problem

A high-volume foundry was pouring at capacity but flying blind between the melt and the money. Heat records lived in registers, rejection data was tallied weekly at best, and finance saw production numbers only after month-end. When a customer raised a quality claim, tracing which heat a casting came from took days of paging through registers.

# The Approach

Foundries are unforgiving environments for software — dust, heat, three shifts, and operators who have better things to do than fight a form. So the deployment was designed floor-first: minimal data entry at the point of work, with the system inferring everything it can.

# The Solution

- **Heat-wise traceability.** Every pour is logged with its composition, and every casting batch links back to its heat — a customer claim now traces in minutes.
- **Rejection captured where it happens.** Fettling and inspection record rejections by cause at the station, giving the quality team a live Pareto instead of a weekly tally.
- **Melt-to-margin visibility.** Material consumption, power, and rejection costs land against actual production, so finance sees foundry margins [daily/weekly] instead of at month-end.
- **Dispatch and compliance in the flow.** Invoices, e-way bills, and GST fall out of the dispatch process automatically.
